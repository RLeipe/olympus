# Roman: training context

Read this at the start of every planning session. Keep it current and short.

## Goals
- Main goal: consistency and fun. General health and the feeling of having done the daily
  training matter more than peak performance.
- Next race: Rennsteiglauf marathon, May 2027 (hilly, partly trail). No other race planned.
- Strength: fix the weak posterior chain and posture ("shrimp posture"); 10 strict
  pull-ups in one set.

## Constraints and preferences
- 5-6 sessions per week, roughly half strength, half running. Trains in the morning
  before work, about 45 min per session.
- Home gym: rack with pull-up bar and dip handles, barbell, SZ bar. No machines/cables.
- Business travel sometimes: plan a shorter, lighter hotel-room session (bodyweight, no
  equipment) only when Roman asks for it.
- Strength: exact kg in 2.5 kg steps, no %1RM programming. Likes focused days (back day,
  leg day, upper day) over full-body, but not dogmatically. Pull-ups in every session
  where they fit (likes them, good for the shoulders).
- Little bench press / chest work; pull more than push.
- Running: prescribe by distance (sometimes elevation), kind easy / long / tempo /
  intervals. Easy runs by heart rate, just under 160. Pace only for tempo and intervals.
- Running must stay fun: vary the hard session (hills, intervals by distance or time,
  tempo, time trials), new routes, trails.
- Intervals: wants to actually do them; records them with laps on the Garmin.

## Principles
- Week: Mon back & posterior chain, Tue hard run, Wed legs & core, Thu easy run,
  Fri upper body & posture (no leg work), Sat long run, Sun rest.
- Blocks of 3 building weeks + 1 easier week ending in a test.
- Paces from the latest race result (half marathon 1:55:45 → 5 km ~5:00/km,
  tempo ~5:15-5:20/km); update after each test.
